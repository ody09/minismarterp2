# Sales and Customer Pricing Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans task-by-task, with test-first behavior and one final fresh reviewer.

**Goal:** Sales select customer prices and confirm one full, auditable FEFO stock issue.
**Architecture:** Add sales documents and customer price rules beside purchases;
reuse StockContext/StockLedger. A small fixed-decimal helper supports exact pricing.
**Tech Stack:** Laravel 13, PHP8.4, MySQL8.4, Blade/static JS, no new dependencies.
**Spec:** docs/phase-5.md (owner explicitly authorized continuing implementation).

## Global Constraints
- Existing MySQL, Arabic/English RTL/LTR, preserve user data/credentials/custom roles.
- Quantities integer pieces; price6 decimals/max999999999.999999, total max999999999999.999999.
- No new stock adjustments, manual opening, partial fulfilment, returns, payments, AI.
- Admin anchor -> warehouse -> current actor/permissions -> order -> customer ->
  products sorted -> prices -> stock. Invariant reads must be current locking reads.

## Review Focus
- Parallel confirmations, competing orders, cancel/edit races cannot double issue or oversell.
- Old RR snapshots cannot hide fresh drafts, changed prices, revoked actors or permissions.
- Date boundaries, open periods, zero price, unit conversion and tier choice must be deterministic.
- Split FEFO allocations remain linked to their actual sale line; expired stock cannot be used.
- Bad arrays, forged prices/customer IDs, stale forms, overflow or mid-write failure never partly post.
